CRUMBLE!

The floor doesn't wait for you.

A 12-player Roblox knockback brawler on a collapsing 3D arena. There are no health bars: you knock rivals into the void. Weapons range from Common to Mythic, and higher rarity means harder knockback. Merge duplicates to level a weapon from L1 to L10 for even more knockback. Timing never changes: every weapon keeps the same windup, cooldown and range at every rarity and level, so the better read still wins.

v3.1 — PLAYTEST CHANGES (from build v0.5)

What the first playtest changed.

The first build (v0.5 Scale) showed three problems. These changes replace the older numbers everywhere in this document and are already in build v0.5.1.

Problem foundCauseChange
Holes didn't kill. You could walk straight across a gap without falling, and bots didn't fall in either.A 4-stud hole is smaller than a character's stride. At walk speed 20 you're over the gap for about 0.1 s, then the Humanoid steps back up.Tiles are now 8×8 studs on a 28×28 grid (the arena is still 224 studs across). Every hole is 8 studs wide, which takes about 0.3 s to cross and drops you about 9 studs, so you fall unless you jump. Crack warning goes from 1.15 s to 1.4 s because tiles are bigger. Bonus: 784 parts instead of 3,136, which helps server FPS and phones.
Players could climb back up the sides of tiles.The Humanoid's Climbing state treats tile edges and seams like a ladder.Climbing is disabled for every player and bot. Once you're below the floor surface, you're falling.
No pressure from the edges. Random holes alone didn't push players together.Only Sudden Death closed the edge, and only for the last 30 s.New: Shrinking edge. The whole outer ring flashes for 2.5 s, then drops together, all round long and faster each phase (see section 08). Random holes still happen inside.
Shield change (requested).−60% made blocking feel weak.Block now stops 90% of knockback, but guard is a resource: it drains while held (5/s), each blocked hit drains base KB × 4, it regenerates slowly (8/s after 2 s, so 12.5 s from empty to full), you need 15 guard to raise it again, and the hit that breaks it lands at full knockback with a 1 s stun (see section 09).

v3.2: what building v0.6 Power changed

TopicDecision
Spin Vault arrives in v0.6Copies need a source, so the Vault (earned spins and coin spins) ships with the Forge. Robux spin products stay in v0.9 Monetization.
WildcardsWildcards are a per-rarity balance, made at the Forge's Wildcard Bench: 2 copies of a weapon → 1 wildcard of that rarity, or 3 copies → 1 wildcard of the next rarity. A wildcard counts as a copy of any weapon of its rarity. Merging uses your own copies first, then wildcards. Converting is always the player's choice, so the game never spends copies of one weapon to level another without asking.
Effective odds with pityMeasured over 300,000 spins of the real code: Common 53.5%, Uncommon 25.3%, Rare 11.6%, Epic 6.3%, Legendary 2.5%, Mythic 0.84%. The Vault shows the base odds plus live pity counters. The effective rates go on the odds info page.
Training RangeA lane in the west of the Yard with three dummies on rails. They fly exactly as far as a real hit would (same formula, same launch physics), then show the measured distance in studs and glide back. A Preview next level toggle lets players feel a merge before paying for it.
Starter quests (pulled forward from v0.9)Survive 60 s → 200 coins · Knock 1 rival off → 150 coins · Finish top 3 → +1 spin. Together with Boot Training L1→2 (150 coins), a new player reaches the Forge and the Vault within their first few rounds.
Acceptance test passed"Merges are atomic; 0 lost items in 1,000 test merges": a fuzz test ran 40,000 random requests including 1,198 merges against the real server code. Every merge cost exactly the listed copies and coins, every rejected request left the profile unchanged, and there were 0 violations. Pity never exceeded 20 / 60 / 200.

v3.4: what the v0.7.1 Polish review changed

TopicDecision
Clear power ladder (replaces the overlap model)Playtesters found Mythic too weak: Void Hook had 0 base knockback, so the rarity bonus multiplied nothing, and Bomb Bag (11) hit softer than the free Boot (14). Rarity bonuses are now C ×1.00 · U ×1.12 · R ×1.25 · E ×1.40 · L ×1.60 · M ×1.80, plus 4% per level, and the KB cap rose from 28 to 40. Each rarity's strongest single hit beats the tier below at both L1 and L10, and an automated test checks this on every build (table in section 10). PWR now runs 100–216 (v3.8: Legendary/Mythic trimmed to ×1.50/×1.60 and the cap to 34, so PWR runs 100–196).
Void Hook reworkThe Void Hook no longer drags both players to a meeting point. It yanks the target to 4 studs in front of you, then slams them away 0.4 s later (base 20 KB: 36 at L1, 40 when capped), with an 8 s cooldown. A parry during the windup cancels the yank. Block still cuts the slam by 90%.
Guard drain 4 → 3 per base KBThe comment said a Boot "breaks guard on the 3rd hit", but with Boot at 14 base KB it actually broke on the 2nd. Drain is now base KB × 3, so a Boot breaks a full guard on the 3rd blocked hit and a Void Hook on the 2nd. This keeps Block worth holding now that the mid tiers hit harder.
Mid-tier retuneSlingshot 9→11, Bat 13→15, Pan 10→14, Magnet 8→13 pull, Bomb Bag 11→16. Timing is unchanged, so skill rules (windup, cooldown, range) still separate the weapons.
Mythic still loses to good playMythic beats a fresh player in one or two clean hits, but the 0.4 s yank windup is readable and parryable. Block −90% applies to every hit, and guard drain uses base KB, so a Mythic doesn't break guards faster than its weapon type. Matchmaking brackets were widened to the new PWR range: Rookie ≤120, Standard 121–160, Elite 161+.
Join prompt before every matchNo automatic pull-in. Every lobby player sees a "NEXT MATCH" card for 15 s with JOIN! [J] and SIT OUT [N] buttons. Not answering means sitting out, and there is no auto-join toggle, so AFK players never get thrown in. The window closes early once everyone has answered. Bots fill every seat not taken by a player who joined (12 total). If nobody joins, the game asks again after 6 s. Answers can be changed until the window closes. This replaces the old "minimum 2 humans / solo wait" rule: one player who says yes is enough to start a match.
Separate windows, not one tabbed menuWeapons ⚔️, Spin Vault 🎰 and Forge 🔨 each have their own round dock button (left middle of the screen) and their own window, with a title tab, wallet and close button. Opening one closes the other, so screen space never stacks. B toggles Weapons. Quests moved to the bottom-right, away from the kill feed.
Rounded art directionNo sharp boxes anywhere the player looks: see "Bubbly Brawler" below. The only exception is the arena tiles. They stay crisp 8-stud squares because they're the gameplay grid: holes must line up exactly, and a rounded tile would leave gaps that read as holes.

v4.8: dummies, long-range assist & HUD layout (v0.9.3). These override older rows.

TopicDecision
Range dummies (root cause)The heaps came from force-limited Avatar Joint Upgrade joints on script-built rigs (AnimationConstraint with IsKinematic = false): without a live Humanoid balancing them, the limbs sag even with an anchored root. All bot and dummy rigs now get kinematic joints (they behave like Motor6Ds). Dummy flight is kinematic: the root stays anchored and follows the ballistic arc of the launch velocity (workspace gravity, landing at post height). It tips back up to 90° (plus one full flip on big hits), lands lying flat, stays down 0.35 s, stands up at the landing spot (0.4 s Back ease), and glides home after ResetDelay. If it lands outside the yard, it snaps back to its post. Watchdog: if the head ends up at or below the hips, or more than 6 studs from the root, the dummy is rebuilt (max 3 per dummy).
Long-range aim assistApplies to Slingshot (projectile), Bomb Bag (thrown), Magnet (pull) and Void Hook (tether). Tracking reach = weapon Range + body radius + 1, inside a ±40° cone (melee keeps ±55°, reach + 5). The Slingshot leads the target's flat velocity (2 iterations, ≤1 s of travel). The Bomb Bag throw distance = distance to the tracked target, clamped to 6 … Range, encoded as the length of the aim vector; the server clamps it, and unit aims (bots, no target) throw at full reach. Ground guide while the weapon is out: a dotted line to the exact reach with an end bar (dots dim past the target), or for the Bomb Bag an arc to the landing spot plus a blast-radius circle. It is yellow while tracking and white otherwise. Server hit rules are unchanged.
HUD layout systemThe client Layout module sets UIScale = clamp(min(w/1280, h/760), 0.55, 1.15) on every HUD block. Top stack (UIListLayout under the timer; only visible items take space): join pill 10 · tutorial 20 · tip 30 · spectate 40 · hint 50 · toast 60. Its width is the gap between the coin/level cards and the right column (340–640 units), and long text wraps. Lobby tips hide while anything ranked below 50 is showing. Right column (top-right): quests 10 (auto-height) or the Range card 20 while in the Range, kill feed 30. The join card and vote sit under the stack, shrunk to fit above the action bar (×0.4 min), and the tutorial card hides while the join card is up. On touch, the action bar is centred in the space left of a lower thumb cluster (SHOVE (−90,−95), RUSH (−200,−60), BLOCK (−185,−165)). The wind gauge is at the left edge, mid-screen. The layout gui is DisplayOrder 4, so menu windows cover it. Verified by tools/layout_sim.py: 0 overlaps across 6 states × 7 resolutions.

v4.7: Lock & Range feel (v0.9.2). These override older rows, including v4.6.

TopicDecision
One aim system: LOCKEDHits (Shove, Block, Rush) always go the way the character is facing, and you turn by walking. The yellow ground chevron shows the hit direction during a match and in the Range. UNLOCKED (camera aim) and every toggle are removed: T, middle mouse, R3, the pill and the mobile 🔒 button. T / MMB / R3 are now free keys.
Aim assist + tracking ringEvery frame the client picks the enemy aim assist will hit: inside ±55° of your facing, within weapon reach + body radius + 5 studs (max 45), and less than 10 studs above or below you. The score is distance × (2 − alignment), and the lowest wins. That target gets a pulsing yellow ring under its feet and a bobbing drawn V marker overhead (only you see them). On Shove or Block, the character snaps to face it and the aim is sent toward it, so the server's hit cone check passes. Candidates: living round players and bots during a match; the Range dummies and Sparring Bot in the Range (mid-air dummies skipped). The server's hit rules are unchanged: assist only turns you, so it never extends reach.
Range dummies stand back upOn landing, a dummy re-anchors and tweens upright at the landing spot (post height and facing, 0.3 s Back ease), and its LaunchedUntil is cleared so the flail stops. After ResetDelay (2.2 s) it glides back to its post. If it lands off the yard, it snaps straight back onto its post.
Fight kit in the RangeThe match action bar (weapon card, SHOVE / RUSH / SHIELD bars) is visible while InRange, and the Range info panel moves above it. RangeService runs the real shield for Range players. It sets the Blocking attribute (shield bubble + guard pose) and the Guard attribute. Guard drains at the held rate, a blocked Sparring Bot hit costs GuardDrainPerKB × 8 (24), it regenerates at 8/s after a 2 s delay, and you can't raise the shield below MinBlockGuard. Leaving the Range drops the shield. Rush in the Range is checked against the server cooldown and plays the dash pose and FX for everyone.
Rush animationA new procedural "Rush" loop (priority: flail > rush > block / victory), driven by the replicated RushUntil attribute (server time; RushTime + 0.12 s). Pose: lean forward 30°, head up, arms swept back, long stride, with a quick arm pump. FX: a white→blue speed streak from shoulders to feet, two side speed lines, a dust kick and a whoosh. The local player sees it instantly (client-side attribute + local FX), and the server broadcast skips the duplicate.
Tutorial step 6"FACE & HIT: AIM ASSIST" replaces "try LOCKED mode": land one shove with an aim-assist target in the Range. The reward is unchanged (150 coins + 1 Boot copy, paid once). Steps: Range → shove ×2 → block → parry → rush → aim-assist hit → join → survive → MERGE the Boot to L2.

v4.6: playtest fixes (v0.9.1). These override older rows.

TopicDecision

LOCKED: every hit (Shove, Block, Rush) goes the way the character is facing; you turn by walking, and a yellow ground chevron shows the hit direction. UNLOCKED (default): hits go where the camera looks, and the character turns to that direction on the hit. Toggle: T, middle mouse, R3, the on-screen pill, or the 🔒 mobile button. Tutorial accessREPLAY TUTORIAL in QUESTS restarts it any time (the reward is paid only once). The step list is: Range → shove ×2 → block → parry → rush → try LOCKED mode → join → survive → MERGE the Boot to L2.

v4.4: fast ice, full immersion, lock-on (v0.8.5)

TopicDecision
Ice = speedThe Ice Rink makes you faster, never slower. Skating top speed is ×1.25 run speed (25 studs/s instead of 20) for players and bots. You push off hard (72 studs/s² toward where you steer; bots 80), so you're at full speed in about 0.35 s and turns grip. Letting go still glides (Glide 0.75/s: about half the slide of v5.9). v6.0 playtest: "too slippery". Was ×1.45 / push 46 / glide 0.35. Blizzard gusts still add on top.
One map, one worldA player in a match sees only that map. On Molten and Ice, the cozy stadium is replaced by the map's own stadium: Molten has basalt stands, ember banners, lava trims and torches; Ice has frosted stands, snow on every canopy and icicles. The Yard's green tree islands are put away for everyone. For players fighting in the match, the lobby, Yard and training range are hidden on their own screen. The south side (where the lobby normally sits) gets the map's own landmarks. Molten: basalt spires, floating rocks pouring lava falls, three volcanoes, lava fountains. Ice: icebergs, glacier mountains with snow caps, crystal ice arches, frozen waves. Everything comes back on KO or at results.
Two aim modesFree Aim (default): hits go where the camera looks. Locked: locks onto the best enemy within 44 studs (closest and most in front of the camera). Your character keeps facing them, the camera gently swings toward them, and Shove, Block and Rush aim at them. A red reticle marks the target. The lock drops past 60 studs or on KO, then re-locks the next enemy automatically. Keys: T, middle mouse or R3 toggles; R or D-pad right picks the next target. There's a mode pill on screen, plus 🎯/⟳ buttons on mobile. Lock-on is aim help only. Windups, ranges and server hit checks are unchanged, so it can't hit through blocks or out of range.

v4.3: detail & fixes (v0.8.4)

TopicDecision
Between matchesThe world belongs to the lobby between matches. When the results end, the arena, surroundings, sky and weather return to the default cozy sky stadium. A map's look only exists from the moment it's loaded for a match until that match's results end.
Hole readabilityA hole must never look like a hazard tile. Under every arena there is a dark surface (Molten: black crust shelf; Ice: dark open water), so holes read dark. Hazard tiles carry a raised border, a bright core and particles. Nothing glows directly under the floor.
Molten / Ice detailMolten: rim lava runs and drips, rim cracks, corner fire braziers, dead trees, obsidian, steam vents, under-island drips. Ice: rim snow and icicles, corner crystal lamps, snowmen, frozen waterfalls, open-water leads, diamond dust.
Bot reliabilityBots get a re-plan "go" at match start, a walk order every think, and Ice sliding steered by their goal. Server steps are isolated from each other's errors.

v4.2: real maps (v0.8.3)

TopicDecision
Theme ruleEach map must match its real-world theme in colour. Lava is orange and yellow, never pink; the Cozy pastel-purple tints don't apply to the Molten set (no lavender lift on dark rock). The map changes the whole world: sky, sun, fog, clouds and colour grade, for everyone, from countdown to results. It also changes the weather around the camera, re-tints the shared stadium, and replaces the space under the arena (cloud sea, lava ocean or frozen lake).
Classic (was The Grid, v4.9)The plain original arena: the default sky-stadium look (Glossy Pop since v5.0: white/grey tiles, blue core, hazard rim), sunny clear sky and sun motes. No map features: no hazards, no modifiers, no themed props.
Molten CoreA basalt arena over a lava ocean (currents, crust rafts, bubbles, sparks), 6 volcanoes with lava rivers and smoke, 14 basalt spires, and a smoky red sky with a big red sun and ash clouds. Weather: ash and embers. Volcanoes lob lava bombs into the ocean (scenery only). Stadium grass turns to ash.
Ice RinkA frozen lake (glacier sheet, drifts, cracks), 12 icebergs, 6 glacier mountains, the aurora, and an overcast sky with no sun and cold fog. Heavy snow, with a white-out and sideways snow during gusts. Stadium grass turns to snow.
Bot movementTarget range 26 → 80 studs. Walk orders are re-issued every think (Roblox cancels MoveTo after 8 s). Bots circle their target during cooldowns, and drop a goal they haven't moved toward in 1 s. Rule: a bot that isn't stunned or frozen is never still for more than about 1 s.

v4.1: map identity (v0.8.2)

TopicDecision
PrincipleEvery map has to be recognisable from one screenshot, and it has to change what you do in a fight. So each map gets its own floor material, rim, surroundings, lighting mood (client-side, only at the arena) and one signature hazard plus a supporting one. The 28×28 floor, phases, shrink and core rules stay identical, so skills carry over.
The Grid = construction yard (REMOVED in v4.9: the normal map is now Classic with no hazards)Sky stadium plus two tower cranes reaching over the floor and hazard chevrons on the rim. Crane drops: from 14 s, every 9 s (2 at once after 70 s), a 1-ton weight hangs over a tile for 2.2 s (a growing shadow, a pulsing 22-stud ring, a ⚠). Then it slams: a radial blast of 20 → 10 studs within 11 studs (halved when blocking, a new reason to hold block), and the tile plus its 4 neighbours crack (0.9 s). The core is immune. Bots read the shadow as unsafe. Effect on play: zone denial and forced repositioning, plus "bait them under the shadow".
Molten Core = volcanoA basalt floor over a glowing magma underplate (seams and holes glow), volcano spires, ash and a magenta dusk mood. Magma pools: 3 every 7 s (4 after 60 s), sinking 1.5 st/s (about 2.8 s to be swallowed), walking at 30%. Each pool spreads once after 5 s (14 pools max). Geysers: 2 every 10 s, 86 st/s launch, a 1.4 s warning. Eruption at 95 s: 7 geysers at once. Effect on play: the floor shrinks from the inside as well as the edge, so Rush management decides fights.
Ice Rink = frozen lakeAn ice and glacier floor, aurora, crystal clusters, snow and a cold fog mood. Skating Accel 1.5 / Glide 0.3 (about 2.3× more glide than v0.8). Bots skate too (Accel 2.2). KB ×1.2, frost delay 0.4 s. Traps: 3 every 6 s, max 7, 2 s freeze. Blizzard gusts: from 26 s, every 21 s, a 2.5 s warning (the Foreman calls the direction, plus a camera-relative arrow), then a 12 st/s push for 4.5 s on every skater. Effect on play: positioning against the wind matters, and wind-assisted shoves carry people off the edge.

v4.0: vote & hazards (v0.8.1)

TopicDecision
No weapon cratesThe v0.8 supply crates handed out free weapons mid-match, which undercut the whole Spin → Merge → Forge loop (why spin for a Bomb Bag if a crate gives you one?). They're removed. Rule: outside RUMBLE, the only way to fight with a weapon is to own it. RUMBLE is the one exception because players opt into it by vote, and its weapons are round-only and never unlocked. 00 — DESIGN REVIEW

Is rarity-power + merging the better system?

Why it's better

  • Visible progress: players can see and feel their weapon getting stronger, which is the main thing that brings Roblox players back.
  • Rarity means something: a Mythic actually feels Mythic.
  • Duplicates always matter: every copy feeds a merge, so no spin is "wasted".
  • A long chase: 9 levels × 20 weapons gives players goals for a year or more.
  • Spending has a clear point, which supports a sustainable studio.

Risks, and how this doc handles them

  • Pay-to-win perception → a hard knockback cap, timing that never scales, and normalised Ranked/Duel modes.
  • New players getting crushed → Rookie lobbies and Power Rating (PWR) matchmaking.
  • The free Boot becoming useless → the Boot can be trained to L10 with coins only.
  • Endless gaps between players → a maxed Common equals a fresh Epic L4 (both ×1.36).
  • Paid random items giving power → PolicyService checks and fully earnable paths.
Verdict: adopted. Rarity and merge level raise knockback only. Windup, cooldown, range, projectile speed and duration never change. Highest possible multiplier: ×1.76 (Mythic L10). Hard cap: 28 studs per hit.

03 — THE LAWS

Rules the whole game obeys.

1 · The floor eliminates players

No weapon or hazard deals damage. Only falling ends your round.

2 · Power grows, timing never does

Rarity and merge level increase knockback. Windup, cooldown, range and duration are fixed for each weapon, forever.

3 · Power is capped

×1.76 maximum multiplier and 28 studs maximum per hit. Block, parry and positioning always work.

4 · The Boot is free and can grow

The only free weapon. It can be trained to L10 with coins alone.

5 · Spins are earned

Spins come from Robux, milestones, the weekly chain, the season track, events, and (v6.6) the hourly COIN SPIN: 1,000 coins, once an hour, no weekly cap. Bots fill seats up to 12, matched to the lobby's power, and always labelled.

But a Mythic's guard-break hit lands at its full scaled knockback.
PARRYBlock in the first 0.18 s: reflects the hit and staggers the attacker for 0.5 sReflects the attacker's full, scaled KB. Stronger weapons are punished harder.

Juggle decay

Each extra hit from a different attacker within 2 s does ×0.8 knockback (stacking, minimum ×0.5).

KO credit

The last hitter within 6 s gets the KO. Everyone else who hit you in that window gets an assist. A fall with no recent hit is a self-fall.

Knockback cap

No single hit can push more than 28 studs. That's 3.5 tiles, so standing near the centre is always safe from a one-shot.

13 — POWER FAIRNESS

Power you can earn, and limits you can trust.

Power Rating (PWR)

Your equipped multiplier × 100 (100–216). Shown on your nameplate as a small number in a rarity-coloured badge.

Matchmaking brackets

Rookie (account level <8 or PWR ≤120) · Standard (121–160) · Elite (161+). Matchmaking looks in your bracket for 15 s, then widens by one bracket. Brackets only switch on above about 2,000 concurrent players. Below that, bots and crates do the balancing.

Even Ground modes

Duel, Ranked and Shove-Only weekends ignore rarity and level: every weapon runs at ×1.20. Competitive play stays pure skill.

Underdog bonus

+10% coins for every 10 PWR you're below the lobby average (max +30%).

Giant Slayer

Knock out a player with 30+ more PWR than you: +3 coins and +50 MatchScore, with a special kill-feed banner.

Crates

Always spawn at the lobby's average PWR, so low-power players can grab a temporary upgrade.

Policy: if PolicyService reports that paid random items are restricted for a player, Robux spin purchases are hidden. Earned spins, coin spins, wildcards and Boot Training stay available, so every level is still reachable without paying.

21 — BALANCE OPERATIONS

Keeping power fair after launch.

Expected win rate in a 12-player round is 8.3%. Win-Share Index = a weapon's win rate ÷ 8.3%, measured within each PWR bracket so rarity isn't counted as imbalance.

SignalThreshold (≥5,000 rounds, 3 days)Action
Weapon too strong for its PWRIndex >1.15 within its bracketAdjust base KB or utility. Never touch the rarity multipliers.
Weapon too weakIndex <0.85Buff utility first
Power gap too decisivePlayers with +40 PWR win >3× as often in ClassicLower the KB cap or reduce the level bonus to +3.5%
Boot healthBoot L10 index <0.95 vs. other ×1.36 weaponsPriority Boot buff
Bot win rate>30% in lobbies with 1–3 humansLower the difficulty mix one step
Self-falls>40% of deathsSlow the collapse or lengthen crack warnings
The rule we never break: windup, cooldown, range and duration are part of a weapon's identity and never scale with rarity or level. Only knockback grows.
